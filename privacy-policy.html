Please note: This policy describes KDS's intended privacy practices. It should be reviewed against the services and systems actually used by the business before publication.

1. Introduction

Konexx Digital Solutions (KDS) is a web development and digital solutions business operating under House of Konexx.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, retain and protect personal information when you visit our website or submit an enquiry.

We aim to handle personal information responsibly and in accordance with applicable South African data protection laws, including the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA), where applicable.

2. Information We Collect

When you submit our enquiry form, we may collect:

  • Your name.
  • Your email address.
  • Your telephone number.
  • Your business or organisation's name.
  • The service you are interested in.
  • Your message and project requirements.

We may also receive information you voluntarily provide when communicating with us through email or other business communication channels.

Please do not submit passwords, banking credentials or unnecessary sensitive information through our general enquiry form.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to questions and enquiries.
  • Discuss your website or digital project.
  • Prepare quotations and proposals.
  • Communicate about projects and services.
  • Provide requested services and client support.
  • Maintain appropriate business records.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

We aim to collect and use only information that is relevant to these purposes and to process it on an appropriate lawful basis.

4. Enquiry Forms and Formspree

Our website uses Formspree to process enquiries submitted through our online contact form.

When you submit the form, the information you provide may be transmitted to and processed by Formspree and delivered to the email account configured to receive enquiries.

Formspree operates under its own privacy and data-handling terms. Information may be processed or stored by the provider according to the service configuration and applicable terms.

You can review the provider's information at Formspree's official website .

We will take reasonable steps to understand the relevant arrangements and apply appropriate safeguards when handling personal information.

5. Cookies and Website Tracking

As of the date of this policy, KDS has not intentionally installed Google Analytics, Meta Pixel or similar visitor-tracking tools on its website.

Hosting providers and other website services may nevertheless process technical information needed to operate, maintain or protect their services.

If we introduce analytics, advertising trackers or other technologies that collect personal information, we will review this policy and implement any additional notices or permissions required by applicable law.

6. Sharing of Personal Information

We do not sell personal information collected through our enquiry form.

Information may be shared with service providers when reasonably necessary to process enquiries, operate our website or deliver requested services.

Information may also be disclosed where required or authorised by law.

Where third parties process personal information on our behalf, we aim to assess the relevant privacy and security arrangements.

7. Retention of Information

We aim to retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including responding to enquiries, managing projects, maintaining business records and meeting legal obligations.

Retention periods may vary depending on the nature of the information, whether an enquiry results in a client relationship and the requirements of applicable law.

Information that is no longer required will be deleted, destroyed or de-identified where appropriate and reasonably practicable, subject to legal obligations and the retention practices of relevant service providers.

8. Information Security

We aim to take reasonable technical and organisational steps, appropriate to our circumstances, to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

No online transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We encourage visitors to provide only the information reasonably necessary for their enquiry.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights to request access to personal information we hold about you, request corrections, object to certain processing, or request deletion where legally permitted.

You may also raise a concern or complaint about how your personal information is handled.

We will consider requests in accordance with applicable law, including relevant verification requirements and lawful grounds for retaining information.

10. Links to External Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services. We do not control the privacy practices of websites we do not operate.

We encourage visitors to review the privacy policies of those services before providing personal information.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our business practices, website features or legal obligations change.

The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.
